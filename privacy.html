1. Apa ProCoin

ProCoin ialah app indicator untuk coin crypto besar. Ia memaparkan harga, carta, bacaan teknikal dan tajuk berita. Ia bukan nasihat pelaburan dan bukan cadangan beli atau jual. ProCoin tidak menilai status syariah coin.

2. Data yang kami simpan

ProCoin tiada akaun dan tiada pelayan pengguna. Watchlist, Robot (Manual & Auto), Portfolio, amaran dan tetapan disimpan dalam telefon anda sahaja. Kami tidak menerima apa-apa data peribadi.

Kami tidak meminta kata laluan, nombor kad atau akaun bank, dan tidak memasang iklan atau penjejak pihak ketiga.

3. Di mana app dihoskan

App dan data pasaran awam (harga, isyarat, berita) dihoskan di GitHub Pages. Pautan berita membawa anda ke laman sumber asal.

4. Padam data anda

Padam data laman dalam pelayar, atau nyahpasang app. Anda boleh simpan salinan dahulu melalui Tetapan → Sandaran data.

5. Terma guna

  • Data harga daripada pihak ketiga dan bersifat indikatif; mungkin lewat atau berbeza daripada harga sebenar di bursa crypto.
  • Isyarat dan Robot ialah ujian maya berdasarkan sejarah harga. Keputusan lepas tidak menjamin keputusan akan datang.
  • Crypto sangat turun naik dan anda boleh hilang semua modal. Semua keputusan pelaburan adalah tanggungjawab anda sendiri.
  • Gunakan bursa crypto yang berdaftar dengan Suruhanjaya Sekuriti Malaysia.
  • Kami boleh mengubah app atau terma ini; tarikh di atas akan dikemas kini.

1. What ProCoin is

ProCoin is an indicator app for large crypto coins. It shows prices, charts, technical readings and news headlines. It is not investment advice and not a buy or sell recommendation. ProCoin does not assess the Shariah status of coins.

2. Data we store

ProCoin has no accounts and no user servers. Watchlist, Robot (Manual & Auto), Portfolio, alerts and settings stay on your phone only. We receive no personal data.

We do not ask for passwords, card or bank account numbers, and do not use ads or third-party trackers.

3. Where the app is hosted

The app and public market data (prices, signals, news) are hosted on GitHub Pages. News links take you to the original source site.

4. Delete your data

Clear the site data in your browser, or uninstall the app. You can keep a copy first via Settings → Data backup.

5. Terms of use

  • Price data comes from third parties and is indicative; it may be delayed or differ from actual prices on crypto exchanges.
  • Signals and Robot are virtual tests based on price history. Past results do not guarantee future results.
  • Crypto is very volatile and you can lose all your capital. All investment decisions are your own responsibility.
  • Use crypto exchanges registered with the Securities Commission Malaysia.
  • We may change the app or these terms; the date above will be updated.
